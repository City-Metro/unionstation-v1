About the station

One station, four operators

Union Station Lego City brings City Metro, Republic State Railways, City MetroLink and City Metro-North Railroad together in one place. City Metro’s rail lines use Tracks 1 to 3, and the Regional Rail Area on Tracks 4 to 7 is home to City Metro Express, Metro-North and MetroLink.

Whether you are heading downtown, out to the Airport or travelling further, you will find your train here. While you wait, visit the Lego store, grab a coffee at the coffee shop or enjoy the other activities around the station. The whole station is fully accessible.

Owned and operated by City Metro

Union Station Lego City is owned and operated by City Metro, a subsidiary of the Republic States Railway and Transportation Authority.

Planning your trip

  1. Use the Trip Planner for City Metro Rail and City Metro Bus only.
  2. For RSR, City MetroLink or City Metro-North, check that operator’s website.
  3. See the departure board for your next train.

Changing trains

  1. Find your operator’s track in the track guide above.
  2. Check the departure board for the next train.
  3. Allow extra time when changing between City Metro and the Regional Rail Area.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns and operates Union Station Lego City?

Union Station Lego City is owned and operated by City Metro, a subsidiary of the Republic States Railway and Transportation Authority.

Is the station accessible?

Yes. Union Station Lego City is fully accessible. Check service alerts for elevator and escalator status.

Which track is my train on?

City Metro Rail uses Tracks 1 to 3: A Line on Track 1, E Line on Track 2 and K Line on Track 3. The Regional Rail Area covers Tracks 4 to 7: City Metro Express on Track 4, City Metro-North on Track 5, and City MetroLink on Tracks 6 (Airport Line) and 7 (TV Room Central Line). The departure board shows the next train for every service.

Which services go to the Airport?

The City MetroLink Airport Line and City Metro-North Railroad both serve Felix Lego City International Airport. City Metro’s A and E Lines also run via the Airport.

Which City Metro bus lines stop here?

City Metro Bus lines 111, 211B and 76 serve the station.

Can I use the Trip Planner for every operator?

No. The Trip Planner covers City Metro Rail and City Metro Bus only. For Republic State Railways, City MetroLink and City Metro-North Railroad, use that operator’s website.

What is there to do at the station?

Union Station Lego City has a Lego store, a coffee shop and other activities.

How do I pay for my trip?

City Metro fares can be paid with MyBrickPass. For the other operators, check their websites.

Who do I contact about a security concern?

Contact City Metro Transit Police at 888-CM-SAFE or text 77777. In an emergency, always call 911 first.
