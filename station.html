Station Guide

Union Station Lego City is a fully accessible station with a Lego store, a coffee shop and other activities. Union Station Lego City is owned and operated by City Metro, a subsidiary of the Republic States Railway and Transportation Authority.

Planning your trip

  1. Use the Trip Planner for City Metro Rail and City Metro Bus only.
  2. For RSR, City MetroLink or City Metro-North, check that operator’s website.
  3. See the departure board for your next train.

Before you travel

  1. Check service alerts for delays or planned work.
  2. Have your ticket or MyBrickPass ready.
  3. Allow extra time if you are changing operators.

Accessibility & safety

Fully accessible

Union Station Lego City is fully accessible. Check elevator and escalator status at alerts.citymetro.xyz.

Safety

In an emergency, always call 911 first. For security concerns, contact City Metro Transit Police at 888-CM-SAFE or text 77777. See the Customer Code of Conduct.
