Operators

Rail and bus

City Metro

Owns and operates Union Station Lego City. Five rail lines and three bus lines serve the station.

  • A Line to Downtown Santa Mooica via Downtown and the Airport · Track 1
  • B Line to North Hollowood
  • D Line to William/Western
  • E Line to Table Central via Downtown and the Airport · Track 2
  • K Line to Asian Town · Track 3
  • Bus lines 111, 211B and 76
