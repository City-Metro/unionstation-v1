Operators

Four operators serve Union Station Lego City. Pick the one you are riding with. The Trip Planner covers City Metro Rail and City Metro Bus only. For the others, use the operator’s own website.

Rail and bus

City Metro

Owns and operates Union Station Lego City. Five rail lines and three bus lines serve the station.

  • A Line to Downtown Santa Mooica via Downtown and the Airport
  • B Line to North Hollowood
  • D Line to William/Western
  • E Line to Table Central via Downtown and the Airport
  • K Line to Asian Town
  • Bus lines 111, 211B and 76
