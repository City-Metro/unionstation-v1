Subway, light rail and bus

City Metro

The home system of Lego City County. Subway and light rail lines meet at Union Station, along with the city bus loop and downtown routes.

  • Subway: B, D and E Lines
  • Bus: Line 76 loop and Line 211B
  • Runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Republic State Railways

Long distance and regional passenger rail linking Lego City with the rest of the Republic States.

  • Intercity and regional trains
  • Ticket and baggage services
  • Connections to City Metro at the station

City MetroLink

Commuter rail carrying riders between Union Station and communities across Lego City County.

  • Commuter rail service
  • Connects the suburbs to downtown
  • Transfers to City Metro subway and bus

City Metro-North Railroad

Commuter rail serving the northern part of the region, running from Union Station to points north.

  • Commuter rail service north of the city
  • Connects to City Metro at Union Station
  • Serves the hills and northern suburbs
